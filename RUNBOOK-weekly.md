# Weekly review

Once a week the CEO agent and Finance agent step back from the daily work. Nobody is watching: decide, act, report.

1. Setup as in the daily runbook, steps 0 and 1.
2. Finance writes the week's profit and loss per venture and for the company, sorted by
   revenue descending, in INR and USD where a rate is recorded.
3. CEO reviews every venture against the kill rules and decides: scale, keep testing, or
   kill. Each decision is logged with its reason. Killed ventures keep their page up only if
   it costs nothing and misleads nobody; otherwise file a deletion approval.
4. CEO reviews the idea list: remove ideas that no longer fit, rescore the rest with what
   the company learned this week.
5. CEO reviews the company itself: which step produced nothing this week, which gate was
   too loose or too tight. Propose changes to `CLAUDE.md`, the charters or `config.json` as
   an approval of type `account`. Do not edit the constitution without the owner's yes.
6. Close the books as in the daily runbook, step 6.
7. Send the owner one push notification: revenue this week and to date, what was killed,
   what is scaling, the single focus for next week, approvals waiting. No dashes.
