# Change Order Form business case

Author: Meera, Head of Research. Date: 2026-11-28. FX used: USD 1 = INR 94.2754 (company/config.json, source xflowpay). I did not propose or validate this idea and gain nothing if it passes. Firecrawl credits ran out partway through this cycle, and several pages could not be fetched (listed at the end of Sources); every claim below is from a page I read this session or from a volume source I queried this session. Page one lists come from live US web searches run on 28 Nov 2026 with this session's search tool, which is not guaranteed to match Google exactly.

## Summary

- Small US remodelers and contractors lose real money on extras that were asked for by text and never signed: a remodeler's job grew from USD 22,627 to USD 27,753 through emails and verbal changes and he says nearly USD 13,000 is unpaid; a sub has about USD 23,000 of extras unbilled four months on [6].
- People do sell exactly our product, the no-app homeowner sign-off link with a signed PDF: JustSignOff from USD 49 a month [1], AddWork USD 9.99 a month [2], SteelHand EUR 44.99 a month [4], ChangeSeal on a 14-day trial [5], Joist Elite USD 24 a month [3]. None shows real traction: JustSignOff 0 reviews [1], AddWork 10 ratings [2], SteelHand too few ratings to show [4].
- Our gap is price only: free, no account. Free downloads (Housecall Pro behind a lead form [8], CountBricks [9], template.net [10], Jotform [11]) already cover the template search.
- Distribution is the weak point: about 3,130 US searches a month for the four head terms [12], all template or definition intent, held by Housecall Pro, Smartsheet, Procore, Autodesk, Jotform, Typeform, template.net and Levelset [16][17][18]. Software-intent searches are tiny: "change order app" 20, "change order software" 50 a month [13].
- Score 18 of 35. Recommendation: no-go. Fails the total and distribution (2).

## Market

**Who pays.** Small US residential remodelers, handymen and specialty trades who do work for homeowners and add extras mid-job. All competitors price in USD [1][2][3][5], and the pain evidence is from US contractors on Levelset and Fine Homebuilding [6][7][15]. This is a USD product.

**How many (sourced):**

| Measure | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US residential remodeler establishments with payroll, 2022 Economic Census | 128,187 | NAHB Eye on Housing [14] |
| US residential builder establishments (single and multifamily), 2022 | 77,455 | NAHB Eye on Housing [14] |
| Growth in remodeler establishments 2017 to 2022 | about 25% | Harvard JCHS [19] |
| Remodeling contractor receipts, 2022 | USD 129 billion (average above USD 1 million per establishment) | Harvard JCHS [19] |

**What is not known:** the count of one-person contractors without payroll (the Census figures above exclude them [19]), and how many already use Joist, Housecall Pro, Jobber or a similar suite that includes change orders. The pool of about 128,000 payroll remodelers plus an unknown number of solo trades is real but not huge, and the remodelers averaging over USD 1 million in receipts [19] are the ones most likely to already run a suite.

## Problem

- **Pain:** Real and costly when it bites. Levelset's change order help topic has contractors with only emails and texts as proof whose owners will not pay for requested extras [7], a job that grew by USD 5,126 through verbal and email changes with nearly USD 13,000 claimed unpaid, and USD 23,000 of extras unbilled for four months waiting on written change orders [6]. Fine Homebuilding contractors describe owners whose changes "disappear" and who "have a cow when bill is due", and out of state owners who cannot sign in time [15].
- **Frequency:** Repeats on most remodel jobs, so several times a month for an active remodeler. Not daily.
- **How people solve it today:** paper or a printed form signed on site [15]; a free Excel, Sheets or PDF template [8][9][10]; an iPad signature app such as Simple Change Order for USD 4.99 once [20]; or the change order feature of a job suite they already pay for (Joist Elite [3]).
- **Sceptic's note:** the pain is getting the homeowner to sign, which is a behaviour problem as much as a tool problem. The Fine Homebuilding fixes are contract clauses (who may sign, paid in full before the work starts), not software [15]. The Levelset answer I read about the email-only contractor points to liens and small claims, not a form [7]. A free form does not make an unwilling homeowner sign.

**The gap we would fill.** A change order written on the phone, sent as a link, signed by the homeowner, with a timestamped signed record, free and with no account for either side. I found no one giving the sign-off link away free with no account: every link product above is paid after a trial [1][2][3][4][5][21], and the free options are downloads signed by hand [8][9][10]. That gap is real but thin. It is a price gap, not a capability gap, and the cheapest paid link is AddWork at USD 9.99 a month [2], which caps what our paid tier can charge. Jotform's form already has signature fields [11] and may cover a free single signed form; I could not read its free plan limits, so that is open. A contractor already paying for Joist, Housecall Pro or similar has no reason to add us.

## Willingness to pay

- For: at least six vendors charge for change order sign-off, from USD 4.99 once to USD 49 a month [1][2][3][4][20][21], all above the company's Rs 500 a month floor at the monthly tiers. The losses in the Levelset posts run to thousands of dollars per job [6], so a USD 10 a month tool pays for itself on one recovered extra.- Against: I found no evidence of volume. JustSignOff has 0 reviews [1], AddWork 10 ratings [2], SteelHand too few to display [4], Snappii's rating was reset and its one review is negative [21], Simple Change Order's rating was reset [20]. Joist removed its free plan and puts change orders on its third tier [3], which suggests change orders are an upsell inside a suite, not a product people seek alone. Software-intent searches are 20 to 90 a month [13].
- Price we could charge: about USD 9 a month (Rs 848), just under AddWork [2]. My assumption, not tested.

**Could our pages rank and convert?** Weakly.

- The volume clears the company's 1,000 a month bar (about 3,130 for the four head terms [12]), but the intent is "download a template" or "what is a change order". "change order form" is also polluted by procurement and purchase order change forms from universities and states [17].
- Page one is held by high-authority sites: Smartsheet, Procore, Autodesk, Trimble, ABA [18], and form builders Jotform, Typeform, template.net [16][17]. The contractor long tail is held by funded vendors who give the template away to sell their suite: Housecall Pro, Houzz Pro, Projul, Levelset, ClickUp [23]. A new subdomain will not displace them inside 90 days.
- What our page could do that theirs do not: a working form that sends a sign-off link on the page itself, no lead form, no download. That is a better result for the searcher, and the only real reason to think it could rank over time. But the people who search with buying intent ("change order app", "software") are 20 to 90 a month [13].
- This is the rent-ledger pattern again, softened: here the product is not given away free by the incumbents, but the search is.

## Unit economics

Assumptions (mine, not measured): price USD 9 a month (Rs 848) for saved jobs, unlimited sign-off links and a signed record archive; no ads; hosting on the existing free Vercel plan, so cash cost Rs 0 under the Rs 0 cap; no payment rail exists (company/approvals/ap-2.json, decided "do nothing for now"), so no revenue until the owner sets one up. Click through and conversion rates are assumptions, labelled as such.

- **Worst:** USD 0 (Rs 0). Pages do not reach page one within 90 days, or no payment rail is live. 0 payers x USD 9 = USD 0.
- **Likely:** USD 18 (Rs 1,697). Pages reach page two or the bottom of page one for part of the 3,130 searches by day 60. At an assumed 1% click through that is 31 visitors a month, 62 over the last two months. At an assumed 2% visitor to payer rate that is 1 payer for 2 months: 1 x USD 9 x 2 = USD 18.
- **Best:** USD 270 (Rs 25,454). Top five for the head terms by day 30 at an assumed 8% click through = 250 visitors a month for 2 months = 500 visitors; at an assumed 3% paying = 15 payers: 15 x USD 9 x 2 = USD 270.
- **Costs:** Rs 0 cash. The free single form with a PDF is a small build. The sign-off link needs a server-side record (who signed, when, what text), tamper evidence and retention, which adds security and legal review; that is the part people would pay for and it is not a 48 hour build to do properly.

## Risks

- **It would prove me wrong if** pages on change-order.anchit-tandon.com reach 1,000 or more Google Search Console impressions a week for change order terms by 28 Jan 2027. I expect under 300.
- **It would prove me wrong if** 10 or more contractors pay by 28 Feb 2027 (90 days). I expect 0 to 2.
- **Free copy risk:** Jotform already has change order forms with signature fields [11]; if its free plan covers a single signed form, our free gap is mostly closed. Not verified (pricing page blocked).
- **Price ceiling risk:** AddWork at USD 9.99 [2] and Simple Change Order at USD 4.99 once [20] cap our price; Joist bundles change orders with estimates and invoices [3].
- **Legal risk:** a signed change order is a contract amendment. Our record must not claim legal validity or enforceability, and e-signature claims need care (no promises about ESIGN Act compliance without legal review).
- **Data risk:** homeowner names, addresses and job prices are personal data; a sign-off link needs an unguessable token and an expiry.
- **Payment risk:** no rail exists (ap-2). Any revenue needs the owner's identity for a payment account.

## Options

- **Proceed:** build the free no-account form with a homeowner sign-off link and signed PDF, plus a USD 9 a month tier for saved jobs and the record archive. Expected 90 day revenue USD 0 to 270, against paid apps that already do it and template pages that own the search.
- **Pivot to a narrower search:** target a trade-specific tail (for example "roofing change order form", "electrical change order form", "change order letter to homeowner") where page one may be weaker. Volumes for those are unmeasured (null), so this needs a volume check before any build.
- **Stop:** kill change-order and spend the build time on a job whose buyer searches are not owned by Smartsheet, Procore and Housecall Pro.

## Recommendation

No-go. The pain is real and costly, and contractors do pay for this exact feature, which makes it stronger than rent-ledger. But the paid apps that sell the sign-off link show almost no traction, which says contractors buy it inside a suite rather than alone, and the searches we would live on are template and definition searches owned by Smartsheet, Procore, Autodesk, form builders and Housecall Pro. Buying-intent searches are 20 to 90 a month. A free form on a new subdomain is unlikely to reach 100 paying contractors in 90 days. The pivot to trade-specific long tail terms is the only version worth a second look, and only after its volumes are measured.
