# Lash Mapper business case

Author: Meera, Head of Research. Date: 2026-11-21. FX used: USD 1 = INR 94.2754 (company/config.json, source xflowpay, 2026-11-14). I did not propose or validate this idea and gain nothing if it passes. All facts were checked live on 2026-11-21.

## Summary

- Lash techs do map every set and some ask how to keep each client's map for fills [1][2], so the job is real and repeats.
- But the exact product already exists, cheap or free: Lash Dash (USD 3.99 a month, 117 ratings, 12 zone map per appointment) [3], LashMap Studio (free up to 20 clients, per zone curl, length and thickness, USD 49.99 lifetime) [4], Lash Map Tracker (free) [5], plus at least five newer paid apps with 0 to 9 ratings [6][7][8][9]. Free web tools (Lash Map Lab, The Face Report) own the "map maker" job for DIY wearers [10][11].
- The 33,100 "lash map" figure in the validation is Etsy search volume, not Google [12]. Google US volume for tech intent terms is small: lash map template 590, lash extension map 480, eyelash extension map 390 a month; tool terms (maker, app, generator) have no measurable volume [13].
- There is no payment rail (ap-2 decided "do nothing for now"), so 90 day revenue is INR 0 until one exists.
- Score 14 of 35. Recommendation: no-go.

## Market

**Who pays.** A working eyelash extension technician, mostly in the US, who wants a record of each client's map. Not the client and not DIY cluster wearers (they are served free by Lash Map Lab and The Face Report [10][11]).

| US lash extension market | USD 361 million in 2025, est. USD 382 million in 2026 (vendor estimate) | [16] |
| Where "lash map" searches come from (Etsy) | 67% United States | [12] |

Market and currency match: the buyer is in the US and pays in USD. The number of lash techs who would pay for a separate mapping tool is unknown. The apps that sell exactly this show 0 to 117 App Store ratings [3][4][6][7][8][9], which suggests the paying pool reached so far is in the hundreds, not tens of thousands. That last sentence is my inference, not a sourced count.

## Problem

- **Pain:** Moderate at best. A tech who forgets a client's map can re-map at the chair; it costs minutes, not money. Techs do ask how others keep client maps for fills [1] and which apps track client lash records [2], so some want a better record.
- **Frequency:** Repeats. Every fill (the clients' own record cards show 1 to 4 week fill times) [17].
- **Current workaround:** Paper client record cards and printable templates (Etsy USD 3.47) [18][17], free apps [4][5], booking apps, or memory.

## Willingness to pay

- **Paid apps exist, few users pay.** Lash Dash at USD 3.99/mo has 117 ratings after 8 years [3]; every newer paid app has 0 to 9 ratings [4][6][7][8][9]. No revenue figure is public for any of them.
- **Procreate and printable packs sell.** Etsy lash map listings run USD 0.99 to 120 [12]; an Etsy Procreate lash map set lists at USD 239.21 with 73 reviews and a Lash Mapping Handbook shows 2.5k reviews (from the validation evidence, Etsy search page) [25]; Lost Artistry Lash sells a Procreate map pack at CAD 70 [26]. These are education and art assets, not record tools.
- **Low price anchor.** The direct substitutes cost USD 3.99/mo [3], USD 49.99 lifetime or free for 20 clients [4], or nothing [5][10].
- Evidence is in USD. A one time unlock around USD 9.99 (Lash Create's lowest unlock [7]) is the realistic ceiling for a manual web mapper.

**Could our page rank and convert?** The head terms (22,200) are owned by Pinterest, Lemon8 and lash brands, and their intent is mixed with DIY cluster wearers, who will not pay a pro tool. The pro intent terms total about 1,550 a month (590 + 480 + 390 + 90) and two direct competitors already target them with free tools and guides (Lash Map Lab, Lash Create) [22][21]. A new subdomain could reach page one for the long tail in months, not weeks, and the people who arrive are mostly looking for a free template, which Lash Map Lab gives away. The searches with tool intent have no measurable volume. Ranking is plausible on the template tail; converting to a paid record tool is not.

## Unit economics

Price is not set (priceINR is null). For the arithmetic I assume a one time unlock of USD 9.99 = INR 941.81 (9.99 x 94.2754), matching Lash Create's lowest unlock [7]. Rankings, click rates and conversion are assumptions, labelled as such.

| Case | Assumptions | 90 day revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Worst | No payment rail in the window (current state, ap-2 "do nothing for now") | INR 0 |
| Likely | Rail opens; we reach positions 5 to 10 on the four pro terms (1,550 searches a month) for the last 30 days only (assumption); 2% click rate (assumption): 1,550 x 0.02 = 31 visits; 2% buy (assumption): 0.6, so 1 sale x INR 941.81 | INR 942 |
| Best | Top 3 on the pro terms for 60 days at 10% click rate: 1,550 x 0.10 x 2 = 310 visits; plus 0.5% of the 22,200 head term for 60 days: 22,200 x 0.005 x 2 = 222 visits; 532 visits x 3% buy (assumption) = 16 sales x INR 941.81 | INR 15,069 |

Costs: hosting on the existing Vercel team, INR 0 extra under the spend cap. Saving maps per client on device (localStorage or a file export) needs no database. Sync across devices would need one; cost unknown until engineering picks it. Card fees for USD: unknown until a rail exists.

## Risks

- **No rail:** if no payment rail exists by 2027-02-21, revenue is INR 0 whatever the product does.
- **Free incumbents:** if Lash Map Lab adds per client saving, or LashMap Studio or Lash Dash ships Android or web, the only gap closes. Lash Map Lab already saves settings in the browser [10].
- **No buyers:** if a live page and free template reach 300 organic visits by 2027-02-21 and get zero paid unlocks, the idea is wrong.
- **Wrong audience:** if more than half of visitors to the tool choose cluster or DIY options (measurable on the page), the traffic is consumers, not techs.
- **Category:** lash work is near the eye; we must give cosmetic planning only, no safety or allergy advice, which the competitors also disclaim [4][8].

## Options

- **Proceed:** build a web lash map maker with per client saves and a USD 9.99 unlock. Likely 90 day revenue INR 0 to 942.
- **Pivot:** a free printable "lash extension map template" page plus client record sheet to test whether techs (not DIY users) arrive; a traffic test, not a business, until a rail exists.
- **Stop (do nothing for now):** kill it and spend the pod on an idea with sourced Google volume, a paying buyer and no free equivalent.

## Recommendation

**no-go.** The job is real and repeats, but it is already done cheaply or free by Lash Dash, LashMap Studio, Lash Map Tracker and Lash Map Lab, the searches with buyer intent are small, and the headline volume was Etsy, not Google.
